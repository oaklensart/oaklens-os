Your site is live.

Five things worth knowing, then it's yours.

  1. Those photographs aren't yours yet. Your site ships with sample frames so it looks like a real site from minute one instead of an empty shell. They step aside on their own as soon as you publish your own work — there's nothing to delete.
  2. Start in Buffer. Drop photographs in, and they queue up there until you publish. That's the whole loop: drop, publish, live.
  3. The big image on your homepage is set in site.config.js, under folioHero. Any frame in your Library can go there — its "hero line" button copies the exact line to paste. Deploy and it's live.
  4. Your link previews already work. Paste your address into a message to yourself and you'll see the card your site generates. Nothing to switch on.
  5. This console installs as an app. Your browser's address bar has an install button — take it, and the console gets its own icon, opens without browser chrome, and keeps working on a bad connection.
