        Your site is live.

        Six things worth knowing, then it's yours.

        1. Turn on Publish. Publishing saves your work to your site's GitHub repo, so your site needs a key to it. About five minutes, once, and it works from a phone.
        2. Those photographs aren't yours yet. Your site ships with sample frames so it looks like a real site from minute one instead of an empty shell. They step aside on their own as soon as you publish your own work — there's nothing to delete.
        3. Start in Buffer. Drop photographs in, and they queue up there until you publish. That's the whole loop: drop, publish, live.
        4. The big image on your homepage is set in site.config.js, under folioHero. Any frame in your Library can go there — its "hero line" button copies the exact line to paste. Deploy and it's live.
        5. Your link previews already work. Paste your address into a message to yourself and you'll see the card your site generates. Nothing to switch on.
        6. This console installs as an app. Your browser's address bar has an install button — take it, and the console gets its own icon, opens without browser chrome, and keeps working on a bad connection.
        This won't show again.

        Turn on Publish.

        Publishing saves your work to your site's GitHub repo, so your site needs a key to it. Once, about five minutes.

        1. Make your key on GitHub. Open GitHub ↗ The name, the permission and a year’s expiry are filled in. Under Repository access, switch to Only select repositories and pick this site’s repo. Then Generate token, and copy it.
        2. Paste it here. It goes only to this site, which runs in your own Cloudflare account, and is kept there to talk to GitHub. Never stored in this browser, never shown again. Revoke it on GitHub any time.
